Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective immediately

AdhanNow ("we", "us", or "our") is a prayer-time application that announces the Adhan automatically on your Amazon Alexa devices. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights over it.

Consent

By creating an account, connecting your Amazon account, or otherwise using AdhanNow, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app and disconnect your account.

What We Collect

Account information Amazon Login

When you sign in with Amazon, we receive your Amazon user ID and display name. We do not receive or store your Amazon password.

Location Required for prayer times

We store your city, country, timezone, and optionally precise latitude/longitude so we can calculate accurate prayer times for your area. If you use the "Use my location" feature, your device's GPS coordinates are sent to our geocoding provider (OpenCage) to resolve your city and timezone. We do not track your location continuously — location is only read when you explicitly set it.

Prayer preferences

We store your calculation method, madhhab, sect, time offsets, selected Adhan reciters, quiet hours configuration, and selected Alexa devices so we can personalise your Adhan playback.

Alexa device identifiers Alexa only

When you or your Alexa device interacts with the AdhanNow skill, we receive and store the Alexa device ID and Alexa user ID solely to know which of your devices should receive Adhan playback.

Mosque location Optional

If you choose a nearby mosque for more precise prayer times, we store the mosque's name, address, and coordinates. This uses the Google Places API to search for mosques near you.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your Amazon password or payment information
  • We do not record audio from your Alexa devices
  • We do not track your location in the background or continuously
  • We do not build advertising profiles or sell your data to anyone
  • We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking
  • We do not collect biometric data

How We Use Your Information

  • Calculate and display prayer times and the Qiblah direction for your location
  • Create and trigger Alexa Routines so the Adhan, and any optional dua or surah, plays on the devices you select
  • Save and synchronise your preferences across sessions and devices
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service
  • Respond to your support requests

How Data Is Stored

All user data is stored in a Microsoft Azure SQL database hosted in the United States (Central US region). Data is encrypted at rest and in transit using TLS. Authentication tokens are stored only in your device's local storage and are never transmitted beyond our backend.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you disconnect your Amazon account from within the app, the personal data associated with your account — including devices, prayer preferences, and account tokens — is deleted from our database, except where limited retention is required for legal or security reasons.

Data Security & Breach Notification

We are committed to protecting your personal information and apply industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections, restricted authenticated access tied to your verified account, and strict origin and session controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will take prompt steps to contain it and will notify affected users as required by applicable laws and regulations.
